After regular allotment and reservation conversion, any remaining vacant hostel beds are pooled per gender (Boys Hostel: 165 total beds | Girls Hostel: 45 total beds). Branch, year, and category DO NOT restrict seat availability in Spot Round. Seats are assigned strictly to verified, unallotted applicants in order of academic merit and registration.
